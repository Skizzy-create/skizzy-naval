# Licensing

This repository holds several works under different licences.

## Modules (`modules/<name>/`)

Each module keeps the licence of the mod it is based on. Our changes to it are released under
that same licence. The licence text is in the module's `LICENSE*` file(s), and the credits and
changes are in its `ATTRIBUTION.md` and `CHANGES.md`. The table in `README.md` summarises
them:

- HullBreach: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0
- NAS: CC BY-SA 4.0
- Large Boat Parts Pack: CC BY-SA 4.0 and GPL-3.0
- Maritime Pack: CC BY-NC 4.0
- Advanced Weapons Pack: MIT
- MNWS: CC BY-SA 4.0
- Iron Seas KANES: GPL-2.0
- BDArmory-Skizzy: CC BY-SA 2.0
- Aircraft Carrier Accessories: MIT
- The overlay patches for Fishy Weapons (CC BY-SA 4.0) and TBAE (CC BY-SA 4.0)

"NC" licences forbid commercial use of those modules.

Third-party files that come from upstream archives at build time (rebuild modules) are not
stored in this repository. They are covered by their original licences, as listed above.
